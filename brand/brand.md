# DecentAI — brand

## The idea
A threshold with something alive inside it. The frame is the boundary of what you run — rounded, protective, not a cage. The gap at the top is the door: agents are let in deliberately. The dot is the agent, held in place by the structure around it. One line: *AI that does only what you let it.*

## Colour
- Ink `#14161A` — text, mark frame, wordmark on light
- Paper `#F8F7F4` — backgrounds; mark frame and wordmark on dark
- Accent `#0E7C5B` — the dot only. Deeper and quieter than the app's current `#10b981`; reads as institutional trust rather than startup mint. Use it for nothing else in the identity.
- Mono: pure `#000000` / `#FFFFFF` (`decentai-mark-mono-*.svg`). The mark loses nothing without the accent.

## Type
- Wordmark: custom drawn monoline grotesque (paths, no font). Cap height 100 units, stroke 14; "AI" set at stroke 16 — the only distinction.
- Companion typeface for product and documents: a quiet grotesque — Helvetica Neue / Söhne, or IBM Plex Sans where a licence is needed. Never a display or tech face.

## Sizes
- Mark: minimum 16 px (use the supplied 16/32 PNGs or favicon.ico below 48 px; they are optically re-weighted). Vector for everything else.
- Horizontal lockup: minimum height 32 px; sits in a 56 px header at 207×56.
- Stacked lockup: minimum 96 px square.

## Clear space
Keep space equal to the mark's stroke width ×3 (¼ of the mark's height) free on all sides of any lockup.

## Never
1. Never fill the frame, close the gap, or put anything other than the single dot inside it — no letters, no glyph, no second dot.
2. Never recolour the dot outside Ink / Paper / Accent, and never apply a gradient, shadow, or outline to any part of the mark.
